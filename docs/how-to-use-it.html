You switch between them with the "Show me" menu on the left.

1

Pick your area

Type an area name in the search box (like "Abdoun"), or click a spot on the map.

The map is coloured — brighter areas are better places to build. Faded areas mean we don't have enough data there yet.
2

Tell us about the home

In "The home you're pricing," fill in the basics — area, size, bedrooms, floor, finish quality — then press "Get price."

Only fill what you know. The more you tell us, the more exact the price. Leaving something blank is fine.
The screen: Where to build

Is this area any good?

You get one big score out of 100, and four simple bars that explain it:

How pricey the area is
Are prices going up
How busy the area is
How few sellers you compete with
Longer bar = better. A high score means a strong place to build. Every bar is compared against all other areas.
The screen: Land price

What can I pay for the land?

Enter how much you can build, the cost to build, and the profit you want. The tool shows the most you should pay for the land.

It works backwards: money from selling, minus building, fees, loan, and your profit — whatever is left is the land budget. Pay more, and you eat into your profit.

Two things to always remember

The honest rules

We never guess

If there isn't enough data, you'll see "no data yet" — not a made-up price. (That's why some small areas, like 2nd Circle, show nothing.)

Prices only go up

This tool helps you hold or raise your price with confidence — never panic-cut it.
