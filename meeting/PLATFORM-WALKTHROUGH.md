# Platform walkthrough — explain it in your own words

Written so you can read it once and then explain the platform simply to the client.
Audience: a property **developer/builder** in Jordan. Keep every explanation to
"what → why → how it helps you."

## In one sentence
> "It tells a developer the right **launch price** for their units — and shows
> **why** — using real market data, on a map of the whole country."

## The core flow (this is the spine — say it like this)
> "You tell us **where** and **what** you're building → we look at the **real
> comparable sales nearby** and the **costs** → we give you a **launch price with a
> safe-to-premium range** → and we **show you why**, with the data behind it."

Everywhere in the platform follows: **your input → Analyse → clear result + why.**

## Section by section (what → why → how it helps)

**1. Pricing — "Launch price"**
- *What:* the recommended price per m² for the units, with a Safe / Recommended /
  Premium band, confidence, and "why this price" (the drivers).
- *Why:* a developer's #1 question is "what do I sell at?"
- *Helps you:* price with evidence, not a guess — and defend it.
- *Your input:* the "Apartment we're pricing" bar (size, bedrooms, floor, finishing
  quality) → the price updates for that exact unit.

**2. Cost & Profit**
- *What:* the most you can pay for the land, your build cost, profit margin, and
  launch scenarios.
- *Why:* price only matters next to cost — this tells you if the project makes money.
- *Helps you:* enter your plot/build cost/target profit → press **Analyse** → it
  tells you the max land price that still hits your profit.

**3. Opportunity — "Where to build"**
- *What:* a ranking of areas by build opportunity.
- *Helps you:* decide *where* to put your next project.

**4. Market**
- *What:* live signals (property / apartment / land price index, up or down), a
  weekly brief, real news (with sources), and competitor projects.
- *Why:* prices move with the market; you need the direction before you launch.
- *Helps you:* see if the market is cooling or heating, and what competitors do.

**5. Location**
- *What:* what's around an area — schools, hospitals, malls, transport — with counts.
- *Helps you:* buyers pay for amenities; this shows an area's real desirability.

**6. Value my property (for sellers)**
- *What:* drop a pin on a building, describe it → a valuation (whole building or
  per-flat) + cost breakdown.
- *Helps you:* value an existing building from nearby real sales.

**7. Assistant**
- *What:* ask any question in plain language (or by voice); it answers from our real
  data, and if it's not in our data it searches the web and says so.
- *Helps you:* no training needed — just ask.

**8. The Map**
- *What:* the whole of Jordan — every area by its borders, every amenity, prices.
- *Helps you:* everything is spatial; you see it where it actually is.

## Where the data comes from (THE TRUST STORY — say this clearly)
> "None of these are random numbers. They come from authentic sources:"
- **Property listings** (to build the price): Bayut, OpenSooq and other portals —
  52,000+ real listings across Jordan.
- **Government data** (to validate): Department of Statistics (official price index,
  construction costs, building licences), Central Bank of Jordan (rates), Department
  of Lands & Survey (transactions).
- **Maps & amenities:** OpenStreetMap + Overture — ~86,000 places.
- **Materials:** Ministry (MIT) cement/rebar ceilings.
- **News:** each item shows its own source link.

> "Every number on screen traces back to one of these — click and you can see the
> source. That's how you know it's real."

## The pricing methodology (simple version)
> "The many thousands of real listings give the **overall price level**. The
> detailed listings tune the **adjustments per feature** — a higher floor, a better
> finish, a view. Then **government data validates** the result. So the price is
> built from the market and checked against official data."

## How it helps you (the one thing to land)
> "It turns scattered, messy market data into **one confident launch price with the
> reasons behind it** — so you price faster, defend it to buyers and partners, and
> see where the market is going. Less guessing, more evidence."
