AI Co-pilot — A Reliable Advisor, Not a Guessing Machine

A generic chatbot guesses from the open internet. Ours is different. It is powered by our real data, our own pricing calculators, specialised skills, and strict rules — so it behaves like a knowledgeable advisor who has read our entire database, not a machine making things up.

The trust guarantee

It answers by querying our real database and running our decision engine, and it cites its sources every time. If the evidence isn't there, it says "I don't know." It never invents a number. That single rule is what turns AI from a risk into a reliable advisor.

How it stays grounded — the architecture, in plain terms

  • It reads our database, not the open web, for facts — our listings, official data, scores
  • It calls our pricing engine as a tool — the same calculators the dashboard uses, so its numbers always match
  • It has specialised skills — write a sales narrative, compare areas, run a scenario
  • Strict rules — always cite; never state a number without a source; say "I don't know" when the evidence is missing

The ways it helps — each with a mini example

  • Ask anything in plain language — "price for a 200 m² unit in Sweifieh?" → a sourced answer
  • Explains the "why" — "why is Khalda cheaper than Abdoun?" → the evidence, side by side
  • Compares areas & scenarios — "rank my five shortlisted areas by opportunity"
  • Writes documents — "draft an investor memo for this project" → a sourced memo
  • Fetches & confirms fresh web data — "any news on the BRT near here?" → cited, labelled
  • Works behind the scenes — summarising areas, watching for what changed since last month
  • 24/7, scales instantly, no extra staff

What it needs

An AI key (such as OpenAI or Google Gemini) plus the skills and calculators it calls — much of which already exists as our decision engine. Most of the hard part is built; the AI simply becomes a smarter way to reach it.

A phased, low-risk rollout

  • Phase 1 — read & explain: answer questions from our data and engine, with citations (lowest risk)
  • Phase 2 — write: generate narratives, memos and summaries on request
  • Phase 3 — watch: proactive area summaries and "what changed" alerts
This is a decision to say yes to, framed around outcomes, not technology: do we want a grounded advisor that makes the whole platform reachable in plain language, while never inventing a number?

Strengths

  • Makes the platform usable by anyone, instantly
  • Grounded in our data — cites sources, never guesses
  • Writes documents and compares scenarios on demand
  • Works 24/7 and scales without extra staff
  • Most of the engine it needs already exists
  • Rolls out in low-risk phases

Trade-offs

  • Needs an AI key (a modest running cost)
  • Best as a co-pilot on the dashboard, not the only interface
  • Requires clear guardrails — which we build in by design
  • Fresh-web answers must always be labelled and re-checked
